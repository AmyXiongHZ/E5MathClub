# E5MathClub · Math Origins — Where Ideas Begin

**E⁵ Math Club** — We study the history of mathematics: real problems, real people, and the links to modern engineering, data and computation.

- **Open to:** all year groups — no Further Maths required, just bring your questions
- **When:** every week (60 minutes) 
- **Founder:** Xiong Xiaoya

## The E⁵ Way — our five E's

**E**xplore · **E**ngage · **E**nlighten · **E**xpress · **E**xcel

## What we are — and what we're not

This is **NOT** a tutoring club, past-paper drilling, a competition sprint camp, or an experts-only discussion group.

This **IS** storytelling — maths with a plot; open discussion and debate; all levels welcome, curiosity counts.

## Main goals

1. **Trace the origins of ideas** — understand why theorems emerged, not just what they say.
2. **Meet the minds behind the syllabus** — from Newton and Gauss to Turing and von Neumann.
3. **Think, discuss, and present** — build logic and communication.
4. **Connect Pure, Mechanics & Statistics** — see mathematics as one evolving story.

## What we do each week

Spotlight (life & legacy of one mathematician) · Origins (how ideas were born — and debated) · Deep Dives (infinity, primes, codes…) · Media (biographies, films, classic problems).

## The journal — *Archimedes Notes*

Each semester the club writes and publishes one volume (~6–7 members):

| Format                        | Author                      | Length            | What it is                                  |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Essay (`journal/essays/`)     | all members, narrators lead | 400–600 words     | event-driven history, zero formulas         |
| Toolkit (`journal/toolkits/`) | toolkit author + editor     | 650–800 words     | one A Level topic + one worked example      |
| Feature (`journal/feature/`)  | editor-in-chief             | 3,800–4,500 words | research essay, UCAS written-work candidate |

## How this repository works

- One folder per semester: `semesters/<year>-<term>-<topic>/`
- Weekly work lands in `weeks/week-NN-*/outputs/`; revised finals are compiled into `journal/`
- Only **weeks that have already happened** are pushed; model samples and spoiler materials (official cipher solutions) stay offline — enforced by `.gitignore`
- Writing rules & file naming: [`docs/writing-guide.md`](docs/writing-guide.md) · meeting agenda: [`docs/meeting-template.md`](docs/meeting-template.md)
