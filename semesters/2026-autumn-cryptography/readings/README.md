## Simon Singh's official Cipher Challenge archive (`materials/cipher-challenge/`)

Captured 2026-10-04 from simonsingh.net/cryptography/cipher-challenge/ (★ reachable from China).

| File | Contents |
|------|----------|
| `Cipher_Challenge_All_Stages.md` | **All 10 ciphertexts in one handout** (web noise removed, HTML entities decoded; Stage 9 is a uuencode file and Stage 10 is number pairs — characters must be copied verbatim) |
| `stages/stage-1..10.html` | Original pages of the 10 ciphertexts |
| `how-was-it-cracked.html`, `leader-board.html`, `cipher-challenge.html`, `the-ciphertexts.html`, `cipher-challenge-archive.html`, `cipher-challenge-essays.html`, `cipher-challenge-gallery.html`, `the-hindu-article.html` | Background, progress board, press pages |

⚠️ **Spoilers are not in this repository**: the winning team's 40-page official solution report and the 10-stage answer sheet (all plaintexts and code words) are kept offline by the editor and the supervising teacher. Background: the challenge launched with *The Code Book* in 1999, prize **£10,000**, cracked on 2000-10-07; Stage 9 was solved first by Gillogly & Palagyi (interim £1,000 prize), Stage 10 closed out by the Swedish team (per-solver details in `leader-board.html`).

Classroom use: Stage 1 (monoalphabetic) → Week 3 frequency race; Stage 2 (Caesar shift) → Week 1 workshop extra; Stages 3–4 → background for Essay #3; Stages 5–10 → editor / competition self-testing.
